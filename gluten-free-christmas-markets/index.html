Mulled wine, roasted chestnuts and a lot of gingerbread. Here's what's usually easy, what to ask about, and the German words to spot on gift packs. Save this for your next trip ✈️

At the stalls

Glühwein 🍷: Usually fine by ingredientsRed wine, sugar, orange and spices. Ask about versions "mit Schuss" (with a shot of liqueur).
Roasted chestnuts 🌰: Usually fine by ingredientsMaroni are just chestnuts roasted in their shells.
Candied almonds: Usually fine by ingredientsGebrannte Mandeln: almonds, sugar, cinnamon. Flavoured coatings vary, so ask.
Bratwurst 🌭: Ask, and skip the rollThe roll is wheat. Many sausages are fine, but some use wheat fillers or breadcrumbs.
Potato pancakes: Ask firstKartoffelpuffer and Reibekuchen often have wheat flour in the batter and share the fryer.
Lebkuchen: Usually contains glutenWheat or rye flour, and a wheat wafer underneath even the flour-light Elisen kind.
Stollen and Spekulatius: Contains glutenBoth are wheat bakes unless the pack says glutenfrei.
Schupfnudeln and crêpes: Contains glutenPotato noodles are bound with wheat flour, and most crêpe batter is wheat.

Stalls and recipes vary, and many share grills, fryers and boards. When it matters, ask: "Ist das glutenfrei?"

Gift packs: the words to look for

  • glutenfrei: gluten free.
  • Weizen, Dinkel, Roggen, Gerste: wheat, spelt, rye, barley.
  • Gerstenmalzextrakt: barley malt extract, common in chocolate and cereal treats.
  • Kann Spuren von Gluten enthalten: may contain traces of gluten.

Quick answers

  • Is Glühwein gluten free? Classic Glühwein is red wine, sugar, citrus and spices, with no gluten ingredients. Check versions with added liqueur ("mit Schuss") and ask the stall if in doubt.
  • Is Lebkuchen gluten free? Usually not. Most Lebkuchen use wheat or rye flour, and even flour-light Elisenlebkuchen are usually baked on a thin wheat wafer (Oblate). Look for packs labelled glutenfrei.
  • Are roasted chestnuts gluten free? Chestnuts are naturally gluten free, and roasted chestnuts (Maroni) are usually just chestnuts. Shared stall equipment is still possible.
  • Is Stollen gluten free? Traditional Stollen is a wheat yeast bread. Some bakeries and supermarkets sell a gluten-free version; read the pack.
  • What does "Kann Spuren von Gluten enthalten" mean? It's German for "may contain traces of gluten": a precautionary warning that the product is made where gluten is handled.
