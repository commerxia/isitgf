Gluten free in France?
You're gonna LOVE it 🇫🇷🥐

Yes, even in the land of the baguette. Plenty of French classics are naturally gluten free, and "sans gluten" is well understood. Here's what to order, what to ask, and a card in French to show the kitchen. Save this for your next trip ✈️

Naturally gluten free favourites

  • Galettes de sarrasin (Brittany buckwheat crêpes): traditionally 100% buckwheat. Ask if wheat flour is added, and whether the pan is shared with wheat crêpes.
  • Steak-frites, confit de canard, poulet rôti: ask whether the fries share a fryer with breaded food.
  • Moules marinières: mussels in white wine, without bread.
  • Ratatouille, salade niçoise, plateau de fromages: cheese is fine; skip the bread basket.
  • Macarons, crème brûlée, mousse au chocolat: usually flour-free. Check, though, because some recipes add flour or biscuits.

Worth asking about

  • Sauces: béchamel and many gravies (sauces liées) are thickened with flour (roux).
  • Fish "meunière" is dusted with flour, and gratins often have breadcrumbs.
  • Soups and quiches: quiche pastry is wheat; some soups are thickened with flour.
  • Look for the crossed-grain logo (AFDIAF/AOECS) on packs in supermarkets like Carrefour or Monoprix, which have "sans gluten" aisles.

Recipes vary by cook and region, so ask every time and check labels. This guide helps you plan; it doesn't replace your own judgement or medical advice.

Useful phrases

Je suis cœliaque, je ne peux pas manger de gluten.I'm coeliac; I can't eat gluten.
Est-ce qu'il y a de la farine dans la sauce ?Is there flour in the sauce?
Les frites sont cuites dans une friteuse à part ?Are the fries cooked in a separate fryer?
Avez-vous du pain sans gluten ?Do you have gluten free bread?

The restaurant card (French)

🍴 Sans gluten

Bonjour ! Juste pour vous dire que je mange sans gluten et que je ne peux pas en consommer.

Rien avec du blé, de l'orge, du seigle, de l'avoine (sauf certifiée sans gluten), ni panures ou sauces qui en contiennent.

Pourriez-vous prévenir la cuisine et demander qu'en préparant mon plat, ils évitent d'utiliser les mêmes ustensiles que pour les plats avec gluten ?
