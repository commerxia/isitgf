Gluten free in Germany

Glutenfrei on a German label means 20 mg of gluten per kilogram or less. This page covers that claim, the durchgestrichene Ähre, and the words on the pack, including kann Spuren von ... enthalten. Save this for your next trip ✈️

Updated 18 November 2026

What glutenfrei means

Germany follows Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 828/2014. The statement glutenfrei may be used when the food, as sold to the final consumer, contains no more than 20 mg of gluten per kilogram. That is 20 ppm.

A second statement, sehr geringer Glutengehalt, means very low gluten. The limit there is 100 mg/kg, and the food contains wheat, rye, barley, oats or their crosses that have been specially processed to reduce gluten. When you want the 20 mg/kg standard, glutenfrei is the statement to find.

Oats in a glutenfrei food have to be specially produced to avoid contamination from wheat, rye and barley, and those oats stay at or under 20 mg/kg.

The same statements cover food that is not prepacked, including a restaurant or bakery dish described as glutenfrei.

Glutenfrei means 20 mg/kg or less. Sehr geringer Glutengehalt is the separate 100 mg/kg statement.

The durchgestrichene Ähre

The Crossed Grain symbol, the durchgestrichene Ähre, is a registered trademark of the Association of European Coeliac Societies (AOECS). In Germany the Deutsche Zöliakie-Gesellschaft (DZG) licenses it. A licensed product meets the AOECS standard of 20 mg/kg or less, with checks on production. The symbol is printed with a licence code, and German codes start with DE. When the product contains oats, the word OATS appears with the symbol.

Glutenfrei on its own is the legal statement. Plenty of glutenfrei foods use the word and do not carry the symbol. The ear of grain tells you the DZG licensed that product.

Words on the label

Cereals containing gluten have to be named and emphasised in the ingredient list, usually in bold, under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011. You will usually see the cereal, rather than the word Gluten.

A line such as kann Spuren von Gluten enthalten, or kann Spuren von Weizen enthalten, is voluntary. It is about possible cross-contact. Ingredients that are part of the recipe sit in the ingredient list, with the cereal emphasised.

Ingredients that often carry gluten

  • Weizen, Dinkel, Grünkern, Emmer, Einkorn and Kamut. Dinkel is spelt. These are wheat, and they contain gluten. Ordinary Dinkel bread and flour are wheat foods.
  • Gerste, Malz and Gerstenmalz. Malt in cereals, sweets and drinks is usually barley.
  • Roggen, in breads and crispbreads.
  • Hafer, unless the pack is glutenfrei. Ordinary oats pick up wheat, rye and barley during growing and milling.
  • Weizenstärke on a food that is not labeled glutenfrei. A glutenfrei product may list wheat starch when that starch was processed to reduce gluten and the finished food stays at or under 20 mg/kg.
  • Sojasoße. Traditional soy sauce is brewed with wheat.
  • Paniermehl and anything described as paniert.
  • Mehl and Mehlschwitze, the flour binding in sauces, soups and gravies.
  • Bier and Weizenbier, unless the bottle says glutenfrei.
  • Seitan, which is wheat gluten.

Eating out

Bread, rolls and pretzels are the default on the table. Beer is usually barley. Spätzle, Knödel and a paniertes Schnitzel are wheat dishes unless the kitchen has a glutenfrei version. Ask about the sauce as well as the main item.

Ask whether a glutenfrei dish is cooked in the same fryer or on the same floury bench as the wheat dishes. Show the restaurant card in the local language from Is It GF? so the kitchen can read the request in German.

Questions people ask

What does glutenfrei mean in Germany?

Under EU Regulation 828/2014, glutenfrei means the food as sold contains no more than 20 mg of gluten per kilogram (20 ppm). The same rule covers a dish described as glutenfrei.

What does "kann Spuren von ... enthalten" mean?

It is a voluntary precaution: the food might have picked up traces of whatever fills the blank, often Gluten or Weizen, from shared equipment. Cereals that are actually in the recipe are named and emphasised in the ingredient list.

Is Dinkel gluten free?

Dinkel is spelt, a species of wheat, and it contains gluten. Grünkern is unripe spelt. Ordinary Dinkel bread, flour and pasta are wheat foods. A pack is glutenfrei when it carries that statement and meets the 20 mg/kg rule.

What is the durchgestrichene Ähre?

It is the Crossed Grain symbol, licensed in Germany by the Deutsche Zöliakie-Gesellschaft for products at or under 20 mg/kg. A licence code starting with DE is printed with it. The word glutenfrei is the legal statement and also appears on products without the symbol.
