Hidden gluten on labels:
7 traps to know 🔎

Most gluten is easy to spot. These are the ones that catch people out, each with a real product we scanned. Save this for your next shop 🛒

1. Barley malt extract"No wheat" doesn't mean gluten free. Barley is a gluten grain, and malt extract shows up in cereals, chocolate and malted drinks.
Example: Rice Krispies (UK): rice, sugar, salt… and barley malt extract.
2. Regular oatsOats are naturally gluten free, but regular oats often pick up wheat in the field or the mill. Look for "certified gluten free".
Example: Quaker Old Fashioned Oats: one ingredient, whole grain rolled oats, no GF certification.
3. The "may contain" lineThe last line of the label is easy to miss. "May contain gluten" means cross-contact is possible even when the ingredients look fine.
Example: Hula Hoops Original: potato, rice flour, maize… then "May contain: Milk, Gluten".
4. Soy sauce in seasoningsRegular soy sauce is brewed with wheat, and it hides in crisp flavours and marinades.
Example: Walkers Sensations Thai Sweet Chilli: soya sauce powder (contains wheat).
5. CouscousIt looks like a grain but it's tiny pasta made from durum wheat semolina.
Example: Roasted vegetable couscous: cous cous (durum wheat semolina).
6. Wheat by another nameSpelt, durum, semolina, farro, kamut and einkorn are all wheat.
Example: Check "ancient grain" breads and pastas carefully.
7. Stock cubes and gravyMany use wheat flour as a filler or thickener.
Example: Oxo Beef stock cubes: wheat flour is on the list.

Recipes change, so always read the pack you're holding. This guide helps you shop; it doesn't replace your own judgement or medical advice.

Quick answers

  • Is wheat-free the same as gluten free? No. Barley and rye also contain gluten. A wheat-free product can still have barley malt extract, which is common in cereals and chocolate.
  • Are oats gluten free? Oats are naturally gluten free, but regular oats are often grown and milled next to wheat. Look for oats labelled certified gluten free. Some coeliacs also react to avenin, the protein in oats.
  • What does "may contain gluten" mean? It's a precautionary warning that the product is made where gluten is handled. The ingredients may be fine, but cross-contact is possible.
  • Does soy sauce contain gluten? Regular soy sauce is brewed with wheat. It hides in crisp seasonings, marinades and Asian sauces. Tamari is often wheat free; check the label.
  • Is couscous gluten free? No. Couscous is made from durum wheat semolina, a type of tiny pasta. Quinoa or rice are common swaps.
