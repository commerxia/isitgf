Gluten free in Mexico?
You're gonna LOVE it 🇲🇽🌮

So much Mexican food is built on corn. Here's what to look for, what to ask about, and a card in Spanish to show the kitchen. Save this for your next trip ✈️

Corn-based favourites to look for

  • Tacos on corn tortillas (tortillas de maíz): al pastor, carnitas, barbacoa. Ask that the tortillas are 100% maíz.
  • Cochinita pibil: slow-cooked pork in achiote and citrus, from Yucatán.
  • Tamales: made with masa de maíz. Ask what's in the filling and sauce.
  • Elote and esquites: grilled corn or corn in a cup with lime, chile and cheese.
  • Ceviche and aguachile: seafood cured in lime. Served with tostadas (corn).
  • Pozole: hominy soup with pork or chicken.

Worth asking about

  • Tortillas de harina: flour tortillas are wheat. They're common in the north and for burritos.
  • Mole: some recipes thicken with bread or crackers. Ask.
  • Shared fryers: chips, chilaquiles and tostadas may be fried with breaded food.
  • Beer (cerveza): made from barley. Churros and pan dulce are wheat.

Recipes vary by cook and region, so ask every time and check labels. This guide helps you plan; it doesn't replace your own judgement or medical advice.

Useful phrases

No puedo comer gluten (trigo, cebada, centeno).I can't eat gluten (wheat, barley, rye).
¿Las tortillas son 100% de maíz?Are the tortillas 100% corn?
¿El mole lleva pan o galletas?Does the mole have bread or crackers in it?
¿Lo fríen en el mismo aceite que cosas empanizadas?Do you fry it in the same oil as breaded things?

The restaurant card (Spanish)

🍴 Sin gluten

Hola! Quería avisar que como sin gluten y no puedo comer gluten.

Nada que incluya trigo, cebada, centeno, avena (salvo la certificada), ni rebozados o salsas con eso.

¿Podrás avisar esto a la cocina y pedir que al preparar mi comida eviten utilizar los mismos elementos que usan para cosas con gluten?
